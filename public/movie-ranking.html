The Best Movies According to John

My Top 3 movies of all-time.

The Shawshank Redemption

It delivers an uplifting, masterfully crafted story of enduring hope and friendship inside a harsh prison.

The Godfather

It redefines American cinema through a deeply compelling, Shakespearean family crime saga and unmatched character work.

The Dark Knight

It elevates the superhero genre into a gritty, high-stakes psychological crime thriller anchored by an iconic villain performance.
